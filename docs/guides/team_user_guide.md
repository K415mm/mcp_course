# Neptune Strike — Team User Guide (Participant)

Welcome to the **Neptune Strike** Team User Guide. This guide will help you navigate the Participant Dashboard, cooperate with other sector teams, and make critical strategic decisions during the maritime cyber crisis simulation.

---

## 1. Introduction to Team Roles

In **Neptune Strike**, you play as a member of a specialized sector team. Successful containment of the crisis requires coordinate action and information sharing across these distinct command units:
* **CERT Command (Cyber Security):** Analyzes malware, monitors network infrastructure, isolates compromised IT/OT systems, and applies patches.
* **Port Authority (Operations):** Manages vessel movements, emergency docking, pilotage, tugs, and physical terminal resources.
* **Maritime Police (Physical Security):** Secures physical assets, conducts maritime border patrols, manages harbor access, and secures critical landside perimeters.
* **Naval Command (Military Sovereignty):** Directs offshore patrols, monitors naval transits, safeguards underwater pipelines/cables, and coordinates with international coalitions (e.g. NATO, EU task forces).
* **Ministry Cell (Policy & PR):** Handles political communication, drafts inter-ministerial decrees, coordinates with diplomatic allies, and manages public media relations.

---

## 2. Navigating the Participant Dashboard

The dashboard is your central command center during the exercise. It is divided into three primary zones:

### 2.1. Global Information Header (Top Bar)
* **Active Scenario:** Shows your current session status.
* **Phase Progress:** Indicates which phase of the crisis you are currently navigating.
* **Crisis Countdown:** A live countdown timer. When the timer hits zero, the phase concludes, and new events are triggered. Pay close attention to this.
* **Threat Level Gauge:** Indicates the simulated severity of the crisis (Low, Elevated, Critical, Out of Control).

### 2.2. Left Column: Action Console
This is where your team executes actions:
* **Decision Submission:** Use this form to submit your operational decisions to the Moderator. Type your action clearly and concisely.
* **Escalation Trigger:** Use this to formally escalate an issue to national command levels or invoke civil-military assistance.
* **External Communication:** Draft official press releases, advisories, or information-sharing bulletins (e.g., TLP:AMBER warnings) to be shared with other sectors or the public.
* **Confirmation Prompts:** Every submission requires confirmation via a SweetAlert pop-up. This is to prevent accidental or incomplete submissions. Remember: **Submissions cannot be undone once sent to the moderator.**

### 2.3. Right Column: Communications & Intelligence Log
* **Live Feed:** A chronological stream of incoming alerts, intelligence briefs, simulated social media posts, and official announcements.
* **Media Viewer:** Whenever the Moderator pushes schematics, vessel trackers, thermal camera feeds, or news videos live, they will appear at the top of your feed. Click to expand and analyze them.
* **Silos Visibility:** Depending on the scenario configuration, you may be able to see the decisions and announcements made by other teams in this panel. Use this info to coordinate!

## 3. Real-time Interruptions & Alerts

During the simulation, you will experience real-time events that interrupt your dashboard operations:

### 3.1. SweetAlert Pop-up Overlays
* **Live Incident Feeds:** Major incident broadcasts will trigger a centered informational pop-up on your screen. Read the detail immediately and coordinate your response.
* **Operational Injects:** Critical injects (e.g., "AIS Signal Loss detected") will display as a crimson warning pop-up. These represent immediate developments requiring instant tactical decisions.

### 3.2. Attacker Ransomware Takeover (Phantom Modals)
* If your systems are compromised by the threat actor (APT-POSEIDON), the Moderator may trigger a ransomware takeover.
* Your screen will turn black and display a full-screen hacker notification. You must coordinate with the CERT team to restore operations or follow the instructions presented.

---

## 4. Participating in Votes, Quizzes, and Sitreps

Your team will be prompted to respond to strategic challenges broadcasted by the Moderator. When these challenges go live, a notification panel will lock on your screen.

### 4.1. Strategic Votes
* Votes represent high-level policy choices (e.g., "Should we shut down the port completely?").
* Discuss the choices as a team, weigh the economic impacts against the security risks, and cast your single vote.
* A SweetAlert confirmation will ask you to confirm your selection before it is registered.

### 4.2. Quizzes
* Quizzes test your compliance, technical knowledge, and operational procedure speed.
* Submit your choices before the question timer runs out to secure points for your team.

### 4.3. Situational Reports (Open Sitreps)
* Open Sitreps require you to write a brief, structured text response (e.g., "Draft a policy proposal defining cyber-attacks as non-innocent passage under UNCLOS").
* Discuss the legal or tactical nuances and type your response. These are evaluated manually by the Moderator.

---

## 5. Winning Strategy & Scoring

Your team's performance is reflected in your live score:
* **Quality Decisions:** Submitting clear, compliant, and well-thought-out actions earns points (`+5`, `+10`, or `+20`).
* **Coordination Bonuses:** Sharing IoCs (Indicators of Compromise) or requesting mutual aid via the communication tools is highly valued.
* **Avoid Penalties:** Submitting rash, undocumented, or panicky decisions (e.g., shutting down the entire electrical grid prematurely) will result in point deductions.
* **Performance Badges:** Focus on excellence to earn special badges (such as **Cyber Shield** or **Joint Force**) which award bonus points (+5) and establish your team's reputation.
